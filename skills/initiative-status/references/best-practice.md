# Howwe best practice: assessing an initiative

> DRAFT. To be reviewed and completed by Howwe's consultants. This file is the single source of truth for the methodology and is shared by Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot.

## Signals that an initiative is on track

- Every milestone has an owner and a due date.
- Milestones due in the past are completed.
- Activities have been updated recently, so the team is actually working on it.
- Aggregate targets and Goals are moving toward their target at the expected pace.
- The initiative clearly supports a higher objective in the strategic plan.

## Signals that it is at risk

- One or more milestones are overdue.
- Activities have not been updated for several acceleration meetings in a row.
- Work is concentrated on one person.
- Targets are behind the expected pace but the gap can still be closed.

## Signals that it is off track

- Several milestones are overdue or the next key milestone cannot be reached.
- No recent activity at all.
- Targets cannot realistically be reached in the current period.
- The initiative no longer supports any active objective.

## How to give the verdict

- Lead with the conclusion, then the evidence.
- Name owners, not teams, when something needs attention.
- Suggest next steps that can be decided in the next acceleration meeting.
